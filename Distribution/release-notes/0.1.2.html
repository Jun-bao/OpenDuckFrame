OpenDuckFrame 0.1.2

Fixes a local-video playback stall when frame generation cannot keep up.

  • Original frames no longer wait indefinitely for the model.
  • Missed interpolation deadlines keep the original picture; late results are discarded.
  • Engine failures remain visible, and old results do not enter a newly selected video.

Slow-model timing and file-playback regression checks passed. This release does not guarantee X2 output on slower hardware; M1 Pro physical-display and audio/video synchronization acceptance are still pending.

修复本地视频开启插帧后，慢模型阻塞原片播放的问题。补帧赶不上时保留原画， 迟到结果会被丢弃；真实引擎错误仍会报告。已完成限速模拟与相关回归， 不代表算力不足时仍能达到所选倍率，M1 Pro 真机屏幕与音画同步尚未验收。
