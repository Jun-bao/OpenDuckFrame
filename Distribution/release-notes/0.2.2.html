OpenDuckFrame 0.2.2

  • 画质工作台侧栏底部新增“官网”“GitHub”“反馈”三个入口，中英文界面均可使用。
  • 新增简洁的反馈页：支持问题描述、选填联系邮箱，以及单张不超过 5 MB 的 PNG、JPEG 或 WebP 截图；可粘贴、拖入、预览和移除截图。
  • 主动发送的反馈汇总到开发者私有的飞书多维表格。从工作台打开时附上页面中显示的应用与 macOS 版本，便于排查问题；不会自动截屏或上传媒体文件。
  • 发送失败时保留当前内容，并明确区分文字收到但截图上传失败的情况。更新了反馈隐私说明。

English

The workbench sidebar now includes Website, GitHub and Feedback links. The new feedback form accepts a message, optional contact email and one PNG, JPEG or WebP screenshot up to 5 MB, with paste, drag-and-drop, preview and removal. Explicit submissions are collected in the developer’s private Feishu table. Opening from the workbench includes the app and macOS versions displayed on the form. No screenshots or media are uploaded automatically. Failed submissions preserve the current draft, and partial screenshot failures are reported clearly.

This community build is ad-hoc signed, supports signed in-app updates, and is not Apple notarized. Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon.
